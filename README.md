# practice-math

A Claude skill for math practice. It guides you through a problem one step per turn and never hands over the answer up front.

It covers every topic from high school through university, follows Pólya's four stages (understand, plan, carry out, look back), checks your steps by recomputing them, and points to your mistakes without fixing them for you.

## Install

Copy the `practice-math` folder into your skills directory, or upload `practice-math/SKILL.md` as a skill in Claude. The folder name must match the `name` field in the file.

## Use

Ask Claude to practice math, paste a problem, or ask for a practice problem on a topic. To reveal a full solution after at least one attempt, type `show solution` or `I give up`.
