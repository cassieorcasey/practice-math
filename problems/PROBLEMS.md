# Problem bank

36 practice problems across six topics, with two problems at each difficulty level. Paste one into Claude with the skill installed and work through it step by step.

Answers are in [ANSWERS.md](ANSWERS.md). Try the problem first; the skill works best when you don't know the answer yet.

## Algebra

**ALG-E1.** Solve $5x + 3 = 2x + 15$.

**ALG-E2.** Factor $x^2 + 7x + 12$.

**ALG-M1.** Solve the system $2x + y = 7$ and $x - y = 2$.

**ALG-M2.** Solve $|2x - 3| = 7$.

**ALG-H1.** Solve $2^{x+1} + 2^x = 48$.

**ALG-H2.** Solve $\log_2 x + \log_2 (x - 2) = 3$.

## Discrete math

**DIS-E1.** How many subsets does the set $\{a, b, c, d\}$ have?

**DIS-E2.** Use a truth table to decide whether $p \to q$ is logically equivalent to $\neg q \to \neg p$.

**DIS-M1.** A PIN has 4 digits from 0 to 9, and no digit may repeat. How many PINs are possible?

**DIS-M2.** A simple graph has 6 vertices, and every vertex has degree 3. How many edges does it have?

**DIS-H1.** Prove by induction that $n^3 - n$ is divisible by 3 for every integer $n \geq 1$.

**DIS-H2.** How many distinct arrangements of the letters in BANANA are there?

## Calculus

**CAL-E1.** Find the derivative of $f(x) = 3x^4 - 2x + 7$.

**CAL-E2.** Evaluate $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2}$.

**CAL-M1.** Find the derivative of $f(x) = x^2 \sin x$.

**CAL-M2.** Evaluate $\displaystyle \int_0^2 (3x^2 + 1) \, dx$.

**CAL-H1.** Find $\displaystyle \int x \cos x \, dx$.

**CAL-H2.** A rectangle has a perimeter of 20. What dimensions give the largest area, and what is that area?

## Probability and statistics

**PRB-E1.** Two fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum is 7?

**PRB-E2.** Find the mean of 4, 8, 6, 10, 7.

**PRB-M1.** A bag holds 3 red and 5 blue marbles. Two are drawn without replacement. What is the probability that both are red?

**PRB-M2.** Find the population variance of 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9.

**PRB-H1.** A fair coin is flipped 5 times. What is the probability of getting exactly 3 heads?

**PRB-H2.** A disease affects 1% of a population. A test detects it in 90% of people who have it, and gives a false positive for 5% of people who don't. A person tests positive. What is the probability they have the disease?

## Linear algebra

Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$.

**LIN-E1.** Compute $A + B$.

**LIN-E2.** Find the determinant of $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 1 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$.

**LIN-M1.** Compute $AB$.

**LIN-M2.** Find the inverse of $\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

**LIN-H1.** Find the eigenvalues of $\begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

**LIN-H2.** Solve the system $x + y + z = 6$, $2x - y + z = 3$, $x + 2y - z = 2$.

## Geometry and trigonometry

**GEO-E1.** A right triangle has legs of length 6 and 8. How long is the hypotenuse?

**GEO-E2.** Evaluate $\sin 30^\circ + \cos 60^\circ$.

**GEO-M1.** Find the area of a circle with radius 7, in terms of $\pi$.

**GEO-M2.** Solve $2 \sin x = 1$ for $0 \leq x < 2\pi$.

**GEO-H1.** Find the distance between the points $(1, 2)$ and $(4, 6)$.

**GEO-H2.** A triangle has sides 5, 7, and 8. Find the angle opposite the side of length 7.
